System Overview

This comprehensive School Management System is designed specifically for مركز دار السعادة (Markaz Daaru Sacaada) to streamline administrative tasks, student management, attendance tracking, and financial operations.

Key Features

  • 📚 Student Management: Complete student registration with Excel bulk upload capability
  • ✅ Smart Attendance: 4-shift system (Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Full Time) with auto-lock
  • 💰 Financial Management: Track payments, expenses, and generate detailed financial reports
  • Reports & Analytics: Generate comprehensive reports for attendance, finance, and student data
  • 📁 Monthly Archives: Archive and preserve monthly data for future reference
  • 🏆 Graduation Tracking: Monitor and record student graduations
  • 👥 Staff Management: Manage staff accounts with role-based permissions
